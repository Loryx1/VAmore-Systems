Banking

Available now

A complete, standalone banking and economy resource: accounts and transfers, savings and credit, live stock and crypto markets, faction payroll, and a fraud-detection dashboard. One resource, no framework-specific forks.

€40 One-time, per server. ESX, QBCore and Qbox. Configurable through Config Manager.

What is in it.

Six modules, all shipped in one resource. Every one of them can be switched off and retuned from the panel instead of a file.

  • Core banking

    Automatic IBAN accounts, 4-digit PIN protection, ATM and branch deposits and withdrawals, IBAN-only transfers with contacts, and a fully filterable statement.

  • Savings and credit

    Tiered compounding savings interest with trackable goals, plus an event-based credit score with score-tiered loan limits and interest.

  • Markets

    10 stocks and 5 coins on live, server-driven pricing engines, with price history charts and take-profit or stop-loss orders.

  • Faction accounts

    Job-bound accounts with grade-gated access, automatic per-grade salary payouts, and a full accounting ledger, with no manual member management.

  • Fraud dashboard

    Flagged high-value transactions, a suspicious-activity filter, Discord webhook alerts, per-player rate limiting, and configurable daily caps.

  • Configuration

    Every setting, from PIN lockouts to daily limits to which modules run at all, is controlled live through Config Manager. No restarts, no config.lua.

Before you buy.

Banking runs on its own. Config Manager is free and optional: install it and every Banking setting moves into an in-game panel instead of a file you have to edit and reload.
