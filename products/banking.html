Six modules, one resource.

Every one of them can be retuned or switched off from the panel instead of a file.

Core banking

Automatic IBAN accounts, 4-digit PIN protection, ATM and branch deposits and withdrawals, IBAN-only transfers with contacts, and a fully filterable statement.

Savings and credit

Tiered compounding savings interest with trackable goals, plus an event-based credit score with score-tiered loan limits and interest.

Markets

10 stocks and 5 coins on live, server-driven pricing engines, with price history charts and take-profit or stop-loss orders.

Faction accounts

Job-bound accounts with grade-gated access, automatic per-grade salary payouts, and a full accounting ledger, with no manual member management.

Fraud dashboard

Flagged high-value transactions, a suspicious-activity filter, Discord webhook alerts, per-player rate limiting, and configurable daily caps.

Configuration

Every setting, from PIN lockouts to daily limits to which modules run at all, is controlled live through Config Manager. No restarts, no config.lua.

Questions before you buy.

Do I need Config Manager to run Banking?

No. Banking runs on its own. Config Manager is free and optional: install it and every Banking setting moves into an in-game panel instead of a file you edit and reload.

Does it work with my framework?

ESX, QBCore and Qbox are covered by the same build. There is no separate fork to pick at checkout and no framework-specific version to maintain.

What does the licence cover?

One purchase covers one server, one time. There is no subscription and no per-player cost. Refunds follow the policy shown at Tebex checkout.

Can I switch modules off?

Yes. Markets, faction accounts and the fraud dashboard can each be disabled, and the limits behind them are settings rather than code edits.
