Invoices

[ building ]

A standalone billing resource, splitting Banking's invoicing out on its own: create and pay invoices between any two IBANs, with automatic overdue tracking and optional auto-deduction. Still in development, so there is nothing to buy here yet.

What it is meant to do.

Billing between two IBANs, without running the full Banking resource: issue an invoice, chase it when it goes overdue, and optionally let it deduct itself when the due date passes.

Everything here describes intended scope, not shipped behaviour. If you need invoicing today, it already exists inside Banking.
