Config Manager

[ running ]

One in-game panel to configure every VAmore script on your server. Any script registers its settings with a single export call; Config Manager builds the interface, the storage and the live updates around it.

free requires owning at least one other VAmore script · ESX · QBCore · Qbox · standalone

What it replaces.

vamore_configpanel — /vamoreconfig
  • one panel/vamoreconfig opens a single ACE-gated panel with one tab per registered script, settings grouped into clearly labelled sections.
  • settings, not codeSliders and inputs replace hand-editing config.lua. Every change applies live: no restart, no resource reload.
  • locationsAdd here places a point at your exact position. Delete here removes the nearest one, with a clear error when nothing is in range.
  • for developersA script registers its schema with one export call, with no core changes, and stays backward compatible when a script does not integrate at all.

Why it is free.

Config Manager is the surface the rest of the catalogue plugs into. Charging for it would mean charging twice for the same script's settings, so it costs nothing. It just needs at least one other VAmore script installed to have something to configure.
