Configuration is a panel, not a file.

vamore_configpanel — free

/vamoreconfig opens one ACE-gated panel with a tab per registered script. Sliders and inputs replace hand-editing Lua, and changes apply live: no restart, no syntax error at 2am because a comma went missing.

Location settings work the same way: Add here drops a point at your exact position, Delete here removes the nearest one. No coordinate hunting.

What one vendor actually buys you.

honest version

Nothing here is a knock on single-script developers; plenty are excellent. The difference shows up in the seams. Scripts from one vendor share a settings surface, a permission model and a support inbox, so a bug between two of them is one ticket rather than two vendors pointing at each other.

What that does not mean: there is no bundle price, no lock-in, and no claim that the suite is finished. Two resources are shipping today. The rest is in the log above, marked building, and will say so until it is not.
