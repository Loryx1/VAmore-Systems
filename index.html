Every setting, one panel.

Config Manager gives each VAmore script its own tab in a single in-game panel. Sliders and inputs replace hand-editing config.lua, and changes apply live: no restart, no syntax error at 2am because a comma went missing.

Locations work the same way. Add here drops a point at your exact position and Delete here removes the nearest one, so nobody has to hunt coordinates again.

About Config Manager

Banking, the full economy in one resource.

Accounts and transfers, savings and credit, live markets, faction payroll and a fraud dashboard. One build for ESX, QBCore and Qbox, with no framework-specific forks.

  • Core banking

    Automatic IBAN accounts, 4-digit PIN protection, ATM and branch deposits and withdrawals, IBAN-only transfers with contacts, and a fully filterable statement.

  • Savings and credit

    Tiered compounding interest with trackable goals, plus an event-based credit score that sets loan limits and interest per tier.

  • Markets

    10 stocks and 5 coins on server-driven pricing engines, with price history charts and take-profit or stop-loss orders.

  • Faction accounts

    Job-bound accounts with grade-gated access, automatic per-grade salary payouts and a full accounting ledger. No manual member management.

  • Fraud dashboard

    Flagged high-value transactions, a suspicious-activity filter, Discord webhook alerts, per-player rate limiting and configurable daily caps.

What buying from one vendor is worth.

Nothing here is a knock on single-script developers; plenty are excellent. The difference shows up in the seams. Scripts from one vendor share a settings surface, a permission model and a support inbox, so a bug between two of them is one ticket instead of two vendors pointing at each other.

What that does not mean: there is no bundle price, no lock-in and no claim that the suite is finished. Two scripts ship today. The rest says so plainly until it is ready.
