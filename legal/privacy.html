Privacy

This page is a placeholder for a reviewed privacy policy. What follows is a factual description of what this site does today, not a legal document.

What this site collects

vamore.shop is a static site on GitHub Pages. It has no account system, no contact form, no analytics and no tracking or advertising cookies. Nothing you do here is stored on a VAmore server, because there is no VAmore server behind it.

What third parties see

  • GitHub Pages serves the pages and processes your IP address to do so.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces from fonts.gstatic.com, which means Google receives a request from your browser.
  • unpkg serves the 3D viewer script used on the home page and the Banking page.
  • Tebex handles checkout. Opening a checkout loads Tebex code and hands your purchase to Tebex, under Tebex's own terms and privacy policy.

Local storage

The site does not write cookies. Video playback and 3D rendering happen in your browser and nothing about them is sent back here.

Contact

A contact address for data requests will be published here together with the Impressum.
